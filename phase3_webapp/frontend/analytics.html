Research Methodology & Algorithms

The analytics presented here are derived from multi-modal sensor fusion (Sentinel-1 SAR C-band and Sentinel-2 MSI). The Glacier Health Index (GHI) is a weighted composite of NDSI consistency, LST anomalies, and SAR-based surface roughness.

NDSI: (Green - SWIR) / (Green + SWIR)
NDWI: (Green - NIR) / (Green + NIR)
NDVI: (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Temporal trends use a Sen's slope estimator to mitigate the impact of seasonal outlier variances. Land Surface Temperature (LST) is calibrated from Landsat TIRS bands using emissivity-corrected algorithms.
